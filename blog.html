How much does SEO cost for a small business in the US?

By Muhammad Rashid · Updated October 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answerSEO cost depends on competition, location and scope. Small businesses commonly pay from a few hundred dollars a month for local SEO to a few thousand in competitive markets. Always ask what is included and how progress is reported.

What changes the price

  • How competitive your keywords are
  • Number of locations and pages
  • How much content must be written
  • Whether link building is included

Common pricing models

Most providers charge a monthly retainer. Others sell a one-time audit or a fixed-price project, such as optimizing a new website. A retainer suits ongoing work, while an audit suits owners who want a clear to-do list first.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. What exactly is included each month?
  2. How will you report results?
  3. Do you use link schemes that could risk my site?
  4. Can I see a similar case study?

How long before results?

Most sites see movement in about 3 to 6 months. Anyone promising page one in days is not being honest.

Shopify SEO checklist for new stores

By Muhammad Rashid · Updated October 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answerStart with clear product titles, unique descriptions, clean collection pages, fast mobile speed and a submitted sitemap. Then add image alt text, internal links and a few helpful blog posts. Do these basics before spending money on ads.

The checklist

  1. Write unique product titles that include what the product is and one key detail.
  2. Write original descriptions. Do not copy supplier text.
  3. Create collection pages for each product category and describe them.
  4. Add alt text to every product image.
  5. Keep the theme light and test speed on mobile.
  6. Submit your sitemap in Google Search Console.
  7. Link related products and collections to each other.
  8. Publish helpful posts that answer buyer questions.

Common mistakes

Duplicate descriptions, huge uncompressed images and collections with no text are the problems I see most often. Fixing them is cheap and often helps quickly.

How to optimize eBay listings to rank higher

By Muhammad Rashid · Updated October 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answerUse a title with the main keywords buyers search, fill in every item specific, add clear photos, price fairly and ship reliably. eBay search tends to favor relevant, complete listings from sellers with good performance.

What to fix first

  • Title: put the brand, product type, model and key detail first. Skip filler words.
  • Item specifics: complete every field. Buyers filter by them.
  • Photos: bright, clear, from several angles.
  • Description: short, honest, with condition and size.
  • Shipping and returns: clear and fast terms build trust.

Keep improving

Check which listings get views but no sales. Those usually need a better first photo, a sharper title or a fairer price.

Guest posting: does it still help SEO?

By Muhammad Rashid · Updated October 28, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answerYes, when it is done on relevant, real websites with genuinely useful content. Links from related sites can support authority, but mass low-quality posts or paid link schemes can risk penalties. Relevance and quality matter more than quantity.

What good guest posting looks like

  • The site covers your industry and has real readers
  • The article is original and useful, not a thin ad
  • The link fits naturally inside the text
  • You publish steadily instead of buying hundreds at once

What to avoid

Link farms, sites that accept anything and posts stuffed with exact-match anchor text. Google’s spam policies warn against link schemes, so ask any provider to show example sites before you pay.

5 tasks a small business can automate with AI

By Muhammad Rashid · Updated October 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answerFive tasks that are easy to automate: answering common customer questions, capturing and following up with leads, sending appointment reminders, drafting social media posts, and summarizing weekly reports. Always review what AI writes before it goes to customers.
  1. Customer questions. A chatbot on your website or WhatsApp can answer hours, prices and delivery questions and pass serious leads to you.
  2. Lead follow-up. Send a thank-you and next steps automatically when someone fills in a form.
  3. Reminders. Cut no-shows with automatic appointment and payment reminders.
  4. Social posts. Use AI for first drafts, then edit them in your own voice.
  5. Reports. Turn sales or ad numbers into a short weekly summary.

Start small

Pick the task that eats the most time each week and automate only that. Measure the hours saved before adding more.
